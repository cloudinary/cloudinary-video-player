How to read the numbers

  • Same film, different cuts. Cloudinary plays the Big Buck Bunny trailer at 720p. YouTube plays Blender's full upload at the quality YouTube picks. Read the numbers as player cost, not content cost. Media bytes are excluded.
  • The YouTube frame hides most of its weight. Resource Timing only shows the API loader. To see the player app and its tracking requests, open DevTools → Network and filter by frame.
  • Cloudinary's main script was already fetched by this demo page on load, by scripts.js. Its bytes are still counted, but its download time is not. "Player ready" for Cloudinary covers the lazy core chunk and the video. "Player ready" for YouTube covers everything, including iframe_api.
  • Main-thread blocking uses the Long Tasks API, which is available in Chromium browsers only. A blocker extension can change every number.

Which one fits

Cloudinary Video Player

  • Your brand, your page and your data. No third-party logo, ads or end screen.
  • Content that must stay private, gated or under your delivery rules.
  • Product and marketing pages where interactivity, SEO and consent handling matter.

YouTube embed

  • Free hosting and transcoding, with no storage or bandwidth cost to you.
  • Discovery on YouTube itself, when the video is also a YouTube channel asset.
  • Quick embeds where branding, privacy and page performance are not a concern.

The two embeds on this page

// Cloudinary: a <video> element in your page
cloudinary.videoPlayer('player-cld', {
  cloudName: 'prod',
  publicId: 'video/examples/big_buck_bunny_trailer_720p',
  muted: true
});

// YouTube: an iframe created by the IFrame Player API
new YT.Player('player-yt', {
  host: 'https://www.youtube-nocookie.com',
  videoId: 'aqz-KE-bpKQ',
  playerVars: { mute: 1, playsinline: 1, rel: 0 }
});
